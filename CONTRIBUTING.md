# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. Before we can merge your first one, we need your
agreement to our [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). It lets us offer
the platform under the AGPL and under commercial licenses; you keep the
copyright in your work. Background: [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

## Agreeing to the CLA

Put this sentence in the description of your first pull request:

> I have read CLA.md (version 1.0) and agree to it for this and all my
> future contributions to this project.

If you contribute for your employer or another entity, name it in the same
sentence ("... on behalf of <entity>"). We keep a record of who agreed.

## How we work

Read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md) for the conventions: small changes, Conventional
Commits, tests next to the code, `make test` before you open a pull request.
