## What the license means for you

This section explains how we read the license. The license text is what
counts.

**Can my company use the platform without changing it?**
Yes, for any purpose. Using it brings no obligations.

**Can we change the platform for our own company?**
Yes. You do not have to publish your changes. The AGPL asks you to offer
the changed source to the people who use the changed platform over the
network; inside a company those are your own staff, and handing them the
source is not a publication.

**Do our apps, app manifests, agents and settings fall under the AGPL?**
No. Apps that run on the platform, the manifests that describe them, your
agents, and the programs that talk to the platform's API are your own work,
not changes to the platform. Their license is yours to choose.

**When do changes have to be published?**
When you offer a changed platform to people outside your organization, for
example as a hosted service for your customers, or pass on copies of it.
Then those people may get the changed source under the AGPL.

**Can we host the platform for others?**
Yes. Unchanged, point your users to this repository. Changed, offer them
your changed source as the AGPL describes, or take a commercial license.

**We want none of these obligations.**
Take a commercial license, see below.
